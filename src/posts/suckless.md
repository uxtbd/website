best distro to use suckless, whether X11 or Wayland is Gentoo, there's no much debate, otherwise you can also use some of the BSDs.

## step numero 1

get a good distro, minimal ideally, alpine, derive, void, anything works as long as it's relatively modern, unless your hardware ain't good, personally I used both gentoo and aerynos

## step dos

go to [wayland.fyi](https://wayland.fyi) and install whatever wm you want, fuck, try them all!

## paso numero tres

enjoj.

## developer stuff

i heavily suggest using [neuwld](https://git.sr.ht/~shrub900/neuwld) if you write a wm/de, it simply works and it doesn't depend on mesa, some people like that.
